Listening audit

How to do this

Each clip is someone in one district describing a picture in Hindi. Play it, then make the box say exactly what you hear: fix wrong words, delete words that weren't said, add words that were. Keep their dialect and grammar as spoken; don't correct it to standard Hindi. Spelling doesn't matter much.

The highlighted words in Vaani's transcript are ones where all three models agreed on something else. The models are often wrong too, so trust your ears, not them.

Press Save when the box is right (even if you changed nothing). Press Can't tell if the audio is too unclear to decide. Your answers stay in this browser; press Download results now and then, and send the file back when you're done.
